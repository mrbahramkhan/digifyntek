# Pakistan Cooperative Society — Registration Checklist

**Legal base:** Cooperative Societies Act, **1925** · Cooperative Societies Rules, **1927**  
**Authority:** Registrar of Cooperative Societies (provincial)  
**Applies to:** Village Agriculture / Resource / Producers / Consumers / Housing / General societies

Use this checklist when registering a new society or verifying an existing VAC under Forge.

---

## A. Pre-registration (promoters)

| # | Item | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Decide **society class** (Resource / Producers / Consumers / Housing / General) | ☐ | Registrar’s classification is final |
| 2 | Confirm **minimum members** for that class | ☐ | Often 10+; housing/producers often 50–100 (check current provincial text) |
| 3 | Each promoter: **age ≥ 18**, sound mind, solvent | ☐ | s. 7-A style eligibility |
| 4 | Residence / area of operation defined | ☐ | Many non-housing types: same village / group of villages / prescribed radius |
| 5 | Draft **objects** aligned with cooperative principles | ☐ | Economic interest of members / mutual aid |
| 6 | Draft **bye-laws** (see Section C) | ☐ | Mandatory subjects under Rules |
| 7 | Proposed **name** (must include “Limited” if limited liability; “co-operative” restricted) | ☐ | Name not already in use; not misleading |
| 8 | Proposed **registered office** address | ☐ | |
| 9 | Feasibility / share capital plan | ☐ | Promoter contributions toward paid-up capital |
| 10 | Registration fee ready | ☐ | Amount varies by category; agri often lower cap in some provinces |

---

## B. Application to Registrar (s. 9–10)

| # | Document / step | Status | Notes |
|---|-----------------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Written application for registration | ☐ | Prescribed form if any |
| 2 | Signed by required number of promoters | ☐ | With identity proof (CNIC copies) |
| 3 | Copy of proposed **bye-laws** | ☐ | |
| 4 | List of promoters with addresses, CNIC, shares to be taken | ☐ | |
| 5 | Proof of payment of **registration fee** | ☐ | Into Registrar’s account as directed |
| 6 | Proof of promoter **share capital contributions** (as per feasibility) | ☐ | |
| 7 | For **housing**: development schedule, master plan, site plan, utilities (within 1 year of registration in some amendments) | ☐ | Failure can lead to de-registration |
| 8 | Any other document required by provincial circular | ☐ | Check Punjab / Sindh / KP current notices |

**Registrar may refuse** if application does not comply, objects invalid, or bye-laws contrary to law. Refusal should be communicated with reasons.

**On approval:** Certificate of registration + registered bye-laws. Certificate is conclusive evidence of registration (unless cancelled).

---

## C. Bye-laws — mandatory subjects (Rules)

Bye-laws must (or should) cover at least:

| # | Subject | Status |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Name and address of society (and branches) | ☐ |
| 2 | Area of operation | ☐ |
| 3 | Objects | ☐ |
| 4 | How funds may be raised (shares, deposits, loans, etc.) | ☐ |
| 5 | Qualifications for membership & terms of admission | ☐ |
| 6 | Nature and extent of **liability** of members (limited / unlimited) | ☐ |
| 7 | Rights and liabilities of members | ☐ |
| 8 | Consequences of default in payment | ☐ |
| 9 | (Credit societies) Conditions of loans, interest, calculation, max members | ☐ |
| 10 | (Non-credit) Mode of business, purchase, sale, stock-taking | ☐ |
| 11 | General meetings — procedure and powers | ☐ |
| 12 | Committee / board — election, removal, powers, duties | ☐ |
| 13 | Disposal of accounts and books | ☐ |
| 14 | Disposal of profits / reserve fund / dividend | ☐ |
| 15 | Custody and investment of funds | ☐ |
| 16 | Amendment of bye-laws procedure | ☐ |
| 17 | Dissolution / winding-up related provisions if required | ☐ |

**Amendment after registration:** Not valid until registered by Registrar. Often requires general meeting with prescribed majority (e.g. 2/3) + Registrar approval.

## D. Immediately after registration

| # | Action | Status | Law / practice |
|---|--------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Open society bank account | ☐ | |
| 2 | Issue **share certificates** / record in share register | ☐ | Max ~1/5 capital per member (non-society member) |
| 3 | Maintain **membership register** + nominees | ☐ | Open to inspection |
| 4 | Keep at registered office: Act, Rules, bye-laws, member list | ☐ | s. 21 style duty |
| 5 | Constitute **managing committee** / board as per bye-laws | ☐ | |
| 6 | Fix **financial year** and books of account | ☐ | |
| 7 | Plan first **AGM** timeline | ☐ | Within period after accounts close |
| 8 | (Housing) Submit development schedule / master plan if not already | ☐ | Time-bound in some provinces |
| 9 | Inform members of registration & bye-laws | ☐ | |

## E. Ongoing statutory compliance (post-registration)

| # | Obligation | Frequency | Status |
|---|------------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | **Annual General Meeting** | Within ~3 months of accounts close | ☐ |
| 2 | **Special General Meeting** when required | As needed / on requisition | ☐ |
| 3 | **External audit** | At least once a year | ☐ |
| 4 | **Internal audit committee** (housing & producers where required) | Before / with external audit | ☐ |
| 5 | **Reserve fund** transfer from net profit | Each year | ☐ |
| 6 | **Dividend** within legal ceiling (10% or 20% for housing/banking/producers) | When declared | ☐ |
| 7 | **Returns / notices to Registrar** | As prescribed | ☐ |
| 8 | Update member register, nominees, share register | Continuous | ☐ |
| 9 | No loan to non-member without sanction (where restricted) | Continuous | ☐ |
| 10 | (Housing) Development & allotment as per schedule / master plan | Continuous | ☐ |

Default in returns / notices can be an **offence** under the Act.

---

## F. Punjab (Pakistan) practical notes

- Governing law: **Act 1925** + **Rules 1927** + Punjab amendments / circulars.  
- Department: Cooperatives, Government of the Punjab (cooperatives.punjab.gov.pk).  
- New registrations may be subject to **policy bans or restrictions** — always verify current CM / department orders.  
- Agri / credit & thrift: **MCL (Maximum Credit Limit)** fixed under rules/bye-laws with reference to member assets/income.  
- Markup rates for agri credit historically notified by department (confirm current rate).  
- Model bye-laws: use department templates where available; otherwise draft to cover Section C subjects.

## H. Quick “ready to file” pack

Before submitting to Registrar, assemble:

1. Application form (signed)  
2. Bye-laws (draft)  
3. Promoter list + CNICs  
4. Share contribution proof  
5. Registration fee receipt  
6. Registered office proof  
7. (If housing) plans / schedule  
8. Any province-specific annexures  

After certificate issued → complete Section D within first weeks, then run Section E calendar every year.
