SA

Islamic products

Sharīʿah modes for VACs — no ribā. Enable via Institution setup → Finance mode = Islamic / Hybrid.

Ops: Salam · Ijārah · QardMurābaḥah flow
Design: Same Members / Credit backbone. Fields use cost + profit, rent, or 0% qard — not interest rate. Asset must be identified for Murābaḥah / Ijārah. Optional Sharīʿah template sign-off. SBP IsMF guidelines apply if licensed MFB; primary societies stay under Registrar + Islamic bye-laws.
VAC core

Input Murābaḥah

مرابحة

Society buys seed/fertilizer/pesticide and sells to member at cost + disclosed profit, payable in installments.

  • Asset must be owned then sold
  • Price fixed upfront
  • Crop-season schedule
Agri

Crop Salam

سلم

Advance payment for defined produce delivered at harvest. Classic agri finance mode.

  • Quantity, quality, delivery date fixed
  • Full price paid in advance
  • Delivery to society / designated place
Asset

Equipment Ijārah

إجارة

Lease of tube-well, tractor, tools. Rent over term; optional transfer of ownership (muntahia bittamlīk).

  • Society owns asset during lease
  • Rent ≠ interest
  • Maintenance terms clear
Social

Qard Ḥasan

قرض حسن

Interest-free benevolent loan. Only actual admin fee (capped). Akhuwat-style for emergency / ultra-poor.

  • 0% profit
  • Guarantors common
  • Fee ≠ hidden interest
Savings

Muḍārabah savings

مضاربة

Member capital; society as muḍārib invests in ḥalāl activity. Profit by weightage; loss on capital (unless negligence).

  • No fixed return promise
  • Declared profit periods
  • Pool segregation
Partnership

Diminishing Mushārakah

مشاركة متناقصة

Shared ownership of asset; member buys society’s share over time. Used for larger equipment / micro-housing.

  • Profit/rent on outstanding share
  • Schedule of unit purchase
  • Clear exit
