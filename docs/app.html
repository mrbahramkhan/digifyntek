SBP control map (in-app)
  • CIF / CDD — full identity, address, SOF, PEP, risk, sole-prop pack, CNIC photos, docs
  • Account opening — purpose, SOF, turnover, nominee, channels, KYC-linked status
  • Cash — purpose, SOF, third-party CNIC, EDD note for large cash
  • Transfer — purpose mandatory, reference
  • TD — product, tenor, rate, SOF, purpose, maturity instruction
  • Credit — purpose of financing, repayment source, security, risk, Islamic mode
  • Collateral — type, MV/FSV, valuer, ownership proof
  • Cheque books — account, leaves, delivery, KYC confirm
  • Members — CNIC, type, shares
  • Controls — EOD, maker-checker, RBAC, AI/MIS

Official SBP returns, live NADRA, and device certification are institution-side production steps.
