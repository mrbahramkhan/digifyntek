SBP control map (in-app)
  • CDD — name, father, DOB, CNIC, address, mobile, occupation, SOF, PEP, risk (CIF form)
  • Identity — NADRA / biometric with reference retained
  • KYC — case status before full banking relationship
  • Accounts — product-based CASA; pending status if KYC open
  • Credit — product, principal, rate/Islamic mode, collateral optional
  • Instruments — cheque books; clearing queue
  • Ledger — double-entry; trial balance; EOD accruals
  • Maker-checker — approval queue for sensitive posts
  • Access — users, roles, permissions, optional admin MFA
  • MIS — AI reports + fixed TB / portfolio

Official SBP returns, live NADRA, and device certification are institution-side production steps.
