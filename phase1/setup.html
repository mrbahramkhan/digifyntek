Institution setup

Province, society class (Act 1925 / Sindh 2020), finance mode — rules engine loads from this.

1. Province & legal base

Punjab: Cooperative Societies Act 1925 + Rules 1927. Registrar primary. Registration may be restricted by circular — verify current policy. Dispute path: Registrar / arbitration under 1925.

2. Society class Registrar classification

Resource

Credit, goods, services for members. Typical VAC / agri credit.

Producers’

Collective production / labour. Higher min. members often.

Consumers’

Supply & distribute goods; may trade with non-members.

Housing

Dwelling houses; master plan; higher share limits.

General

Not in the four classes above (multi-purpose etc.).

Resource society: Min members often 10–30 (confirm province). Share cap ~1/5 capital. Dividend typically ≤10%. Loans mainly to members. AGM, audit, reserve fund compulsory.
